About Me

I am a Molecular Engineering PhD student at the University of Chicago who loves science. I am particularly passionate about applied physics, computer science/architecture, materials science, aerospace engineering, electrical engineering, and the intersection of these ideas in computational materials/physics. Before going to grad school I did my undergrad at UC Santa Barbara where I worked on theoretical/computational astrophysics studying supernovae in binary star systems with Lars Bildsten and Logan Prust at the Kavli Institute for Theoretical Physics. I have previously worked on computational materials science with Anton Van der Ven and quantum information and condensed matter experimental physics with Ania Jayich at UC Santa Barbara.
